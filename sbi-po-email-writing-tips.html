SBI PO Email Writing Tips

Write a professional email that is clear, purposeful and appropriate for its recipient.

Think about the recipient first

Before drafting, identify who is receiving the message, why you are writing and what action or information is required. Your tone should match that relationship and purpose.

Build the email logically

Use a useful subject, an appropriate salutation, a direct opening, the necessary details and a courteous closing. Keep each paragraph focused on one idea.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Writing as if the message were a casual chat.
  • Using vague openings that hide the purpose.
  • Repeating the same request in several sentences.
  • Adding invented facts not supported by the situation.
  • Ignoring a specific instruction in the prompt.
