Common SBI PO Descriptive Writing Mistakes

A final checklist for catching avoidable errors before you submit.

Across all tasks

  • Missing an instruction or part of the question.
  • Using an inappropriate tone for the task.
  • Writing vague filler instead of useful content.
  • Repeating points to increase length.
  • Ignoring spelling, punctuation and sentence errors.
  • Leaving no time for a final review.

Task-specific traps

Email: casual tone, weak subject line or missing the requested action.

Situation analysis: jumping to a solution without identifying the problem, stakeholders or practical consequences.

Report: turning the report into an opinion essay instead of presenting facts and organised findings.

Précis: copying sentences mechanically, retaining minor details or changing the original meaning.

The 60-second final check

Read the opening and conclusion, scan for obvious grammar and spelling errors, confirm that every instruction was answered, and remove one repeated or unnecessary sentence if you find one.
