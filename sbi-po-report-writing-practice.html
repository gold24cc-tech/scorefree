SBI PO Report Writing Practice

Learn a repeatable way to turn facts, observations and event details into a clear formal report.

A practical report structure

  1. Heading: make the subject immediately clear.
  2. Opening: state what happened, where/when relevant, and why the report is being written.
  3. Details: present important observations, facts, sequence and impact logically.
  4. Conclusion: summarise the outcome and, where the prompt asks, give appropriate recommendations or next steps.

Language checklist

Prefer factual and professional language. Avoid dramatic claims, slang, unnecessary repetition and unsupported assumptions. Keep paragraphs easy to scan and make sure every instruction in the prompt is addressed.

Practise under time pressure

Start with untimed attempts while learning the structure. Then use SCOREFREE to practise writing within a fixed window and review relevance, organisation, grammar and word count.
