Précis writing

How to Write a Précis for SBI PO

A précis is not a collection of shortened sentences. It is a concise version that preserves the central meaning of the original passage.

Step 1: Find the central idea

Ask: what is the passage mainly trying to communicate? Write that idea in one rough sentence before drafting.

Step 2: Separate essential from secondary information

Keep the main argument, important causes or effects and necessary supporting ideas. Remove repetition, decorative wording, minor examples and details that do not carry the central message.

Step 3: Rewrite in your own words

Combine related ideas and use concise language. Do not distort the author's position while trying to shorten the passage.

Step 4: Check the target length

SBI's older training guidance commonly describes précis writing as about one-third of the source passage. Treat that as practical guidance rather than a universal word limit unless the current question specifies one.

Step 5: Give it a suitable title

The title should reflect the main subject or argument without becoming a sentence-long summary.
