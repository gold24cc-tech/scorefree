Vocabulary that earns its place

Descriptive Writing Vocabulary for Bank Exams

Use precise words when they make a sentence clearer—not simply to make it sound difficult.

Cause and effect

contribute, trigger, result in, lead to, stem from, influence, aggravate, mitigate

Change and trends

increase, decline, accelerate, moderate, expand, contract, fluctuate, stabilise

Arguments and evaluation

suggest, indicate, demonstrate, highlight, assess, address, justify, challenge

Policy and solutions

implement, strengthen, regulate, facilitate, prioritise, monitor, reform, encourage

People and institutions

stakeholders, beneficiaries, authorities, households, workforce, institutions, consumers, communities

One rule

If you are unsure about a sophisticated word, choose the simpler word you know is correct. Accuracy and relevance matter more than showing off vocabulary.
