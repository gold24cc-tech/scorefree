IBPS PO Comprehension Practice

Build the skill of reading a dense passage quickly, finding its central message and answering without unnecessary words.

A reliable method

  1. Read the question before the passage when it helps you know what to look for.
  2. Read for the author's central point, not isolated sentences.
  3. Mark the key facts, causes, effects, examples and conclusions.
  4. Draft the answer in your own words while keeping the passage's meaning.
  5. Cut repetition and check grammar before submitting.

What usually weakens an answer?

Copying large portions of the passage, adding personal opinions that were not asked for, missing one part of a multi-part question and writing an answer that is far longer than necessary can all reduce clarity.
