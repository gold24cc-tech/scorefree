# SCOREFREE

Free, no-login descriptive practice for IBPS PO and SBI PO.

## Current 2026 patterns

- **IBPS PO/MT-XVI:** Essay + Comprehension, 2 questions, 25 marks, 30 minutes.
- **SBI PO 2026:** Emails (1 of 2), Situation Analysis (1 of 2), and either Reports or Precis (1 of 2), 3 responses, 30 marks, 30 minutes.

SCOREFREE follows the current official 2026 SBI advertisement rather than older 2025 pattern pages.

## Test bank policy

Each practice category contains 50 tests. Reported previous-year questions are labelled **Reported PYQ**. The remaining tests are original practice material and are never presented as PYQs.

## Scoring

Official notifications publish marks, duration and task structure, but do not publish a detailed public writing-rubric formula. SCOREFREE therefore gives a transparent **practice estimate** based on relevance, structure, language and completeness. It is not an official IBPS/SBI score.
