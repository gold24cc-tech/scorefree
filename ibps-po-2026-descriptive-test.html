IBPS PO Descriptive Test Practice

A focused guide for candidates preparing for the English descriptive section. Use it to understand the task, plan your response and practise under time pressure.

What to practise

For the current IBPS PO/MT-XVI descriptive test, practise essay writing and comprehension writing in English. The official notification specifies the descriptive section as 25 marks with 30 minutes, but does not prescribe one universal word limit for every response.

A simple 30-minute approach

  1. Read both tasks carefully and identify exactly what is being asked.
  2. Spend a few minutes planning your essay structure before writing.
  3. For comprehension, answer from the passage rather than adding unrelated opinions.
  4. Reserve the final minutes to check grammar, spelling, punctuation and whether every part of the prompt was answered.
