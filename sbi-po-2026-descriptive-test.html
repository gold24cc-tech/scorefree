What the 2026 test covers

The current SBI PO 2026 advertisement describes a 30-mark, 30-minute Descriptive Test in English. Candidates write an email, a situation-analysis response and either a report or précis task, with the notification specifying two options for each task type.

How to prepare

  • Email: practise professional tone, purpose, clarity and concise organisation.
  • Situation analysis: identify the issue, stakeholders, risks and practical action.
  • Report: use a factual structure with a clear heading, context, findings and conclusion.
  • Précis: identify the central idea, remove repetition and preserve the author's meaning in compressed form.

Use SCOREFREE as a timed drill

Practise one section at a time first, then attempt a full descriptive session. Review grammar, relevance, structure and word count after every attempt.
